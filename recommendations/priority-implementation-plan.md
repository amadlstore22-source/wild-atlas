## Phase 4 — Ongoing: measure, don't guess

- **Benchmark to hold yourself to:** 1–3% visitor→enquiry is typical, **3–4% is top-tier**.
  Track it. Under 1% means a UX or traffic-quality problem.
- Watch the **mobile** number specifically — industry mobile conversion is **−4.1% YoY**
  while mobile is 60%+ of travel traffic.
- Monthly: open GSC, find rising queries, translate the winners, write follow-ups in that
  cluster. **Do not translate everything** — publish EN, translate what earns impressions.

## What NOT to do

Explicit warnings, because generic advice would push you toward these:

1. **Don't rebuild the design system.** It is already culturally accurate and better than
   competitors'. Adding ornament would make it worse. (See `/research/moroccan-authenticity.md`.)
2. **Don't chase 500 articles.** Google's helpful-content system penalises thin volume.
   4 quality posts/week beats 7 thin ones.
3. **Don't auto-translate every new post ×5 languages.** That is 1,000 translations of
   largely unread pages. Translate proven winners only.
4. **Don't re-do the technical SEO checklist.** Schema, canonicals, hreflang, image
   pipeline, internal linking are all done and verified.
5. **Don't panic at "Discovered – not indexed" rising.** Adding 285 locale blog URLs to the
   sitemap necessarily queues them. The trend line is already declining.
