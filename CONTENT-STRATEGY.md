## What I'd genuinely push back on

1. **7 articles/week is too many to keep at your quality bar.** Your existing posts are *good* — honest, experienced, well-linked. Thin content to hit a quota would *lower* your topical authority (Google's "helpful content" system penalizes exactly this). **4/week of real quality > 7/week of filler.**
2. **Don't chase 500 articles as a KPI.** Chase *bookings-per-article*. An 18-article month of itineraries + niche pages will out-earn a 30-article month of "what language do Moroccans speak."
3. **Translate winners, not everything.** Don't auto-translate all 200 new posts ×5 languages (that's 1,000 translations). Publish EN, watch GSC for the ~30 that get impressions, translate those. (This is why the 5 posts I just translated were the right ones — they had ad/traffic intent.)
4. **The AI itinerary planner / tour comparison / wishlist (Pillar 11)** are real dev projects, not content — worth doing, but scope them separately so they don't stall the content engine.
